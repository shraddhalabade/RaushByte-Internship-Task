WHY SLSHOP?

Everyday finds, made easy.

We believe shopping should feel clear, welcoming, and enjoyable. SLShop brings together fashion, accessories, footwear, and lifestyle products in one simple place.

This project demonstrates a responsive multi-page storefront using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap 5, and JavaScript. Product data and cart selections are stored locally in your browser for demonstration purposes.
